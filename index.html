Programmes

Tuition for every stage of school

Classes follow the national KSSR (primary) and KSSM (secondary) syllabus, so what your child learns with us supports what they learn in school.

Primary

Standard 1 – 6

Strong basics in reading, writing and numbers, with patient teaching that builds good study habits early.

  • Bahasa Melayu, English, Mathematics, Science
  • Phonics and reading support for lower primary
  • Homework guidance

Lower Secondary

Form 1 – 3

A smooth move from primary to secondary school, with focus on understanding concepts rather than memorising.

  • Core subjects plus Sejarah
  • Preparation for school assessments
  • Study skills and time management

Upper Secondary & SPM

Form 4 – 5

Focused SPM preparation in core and elective subjects, with exam technique, past-year papers and timed practice.

  • Science and Arts stream subjects
  • Past-year paper drills
  • Intensive revision before SPM

FAQ

Questions parents ask

How big are the classes?

We keep classes small so that teachers can give each student attention and follow their progress closely.

Is the trial class really free?

Yes. Your child can join one trial class at no cost and with no obligation to enrol.

Which syllabus do you follow?

We follow the national KSSR syllabus for primary and KSSM for secondary, including SPM preparation for Form 4 and 5.

How will I know how my child is doing?

Students sit regular topical tests, and we share their progress with parents so you always know where they stand.

What if my child misses a class?

Let us know in advance and we will help arrange a replacement class where possible.
